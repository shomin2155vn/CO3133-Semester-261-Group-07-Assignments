Methodology

Baseline and main methods

We classify each CrisisMMD-MTEB image–tweet pair into one of five humanitarian categories. The four configurations use the same leakage-filtered partitions: 6,002 training, 984 validation and 954 test pairs. ResNet-18 (IMAGENET1K_V1) and distilbert/distilbert-base-uncased serve as frozen encoders. Both stay in evaluation mode; their train/validation features are extracted once and reused across the applicable runs.

RGB → EXIF orientation correction → longest-side resize to 224 → centered black padding to 224 × 224 → ImageNet normalization. Tweets are cleaned by repairing encoding and HTML entities, removing leading retweet prefixes, URLs and mentions, preserving hashtag words, and consolidating whitespace. Tokenization uses dynamic batch padding and truncation at 64 tokens. The attention mask is used by DistilBERT and by mean pooling over non-padding final hidden states, including special tokens.

Experimental design & controlled comparisons

The completed experiment compares modality availability and fusion design under a fixed 50-epoch budget. All runs use seed 42, identical retained splits, the same pretrained feature representations, AdamW settings and validation-based checkpoint rule. Test metrics are computed after checkpoint selection.
