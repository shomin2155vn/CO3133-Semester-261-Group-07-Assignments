Methodology

Pipeline overview

The classification pipeline follows a strict, end-to-end sequential workflow across all experiments on the Food-11 dataset:

Raw data → Preprocessing → Data loader → Model → Loss → Optimization → Prediction → Post-processing → Evaluation
  • Raw data & Split Preservation: The official Food-11 partitions (9,866 training, 3,430 validation, and 3,347 evaluation/test RGB images across 11 food categories) are preserved without re-splitting or automatic deletion. An automated MD5 and 64-bit difference hash (dHash) audit logs exact and near-duplicates across splits to transparently document potential data leakage risk.
  • Aspect-Preserving Preprocessing & Train-Only Normalization: Each image is decoded in RGB and its longer side is resized to 512 pixels via Lanczos interpolation while preserving aspect ratio. Channel-wise RGB mean and population standard deviation (ddof=0) are fitted once exclusively on resized, unaugmented training image pixels (excluding synthetic padding) and cached in train_normalization.json. Images are then centrally padded to 512 × 512 using the rounded training-mean RGB values and standardized via (x - train_mean) / train_std. Training images additionally undergo random horizontal flipping and color jitter (brightness=0.3, contrast=0.3, saturation=0.3, hue=0.05) prior to resizing and padding.
  • Data Loader: Custom PyTorch DataLoader instances group tensors of shape (B, 3, 512, 512) with batch_size = 32 and drop_last = False. Training uses seeded random shuffling (torch.Generator().manual_seed(seed) and deterministic seed_worker), while validation and test loaders evaluate deterministically in fixed order.
  • Model, Loss & Optimization: A custom 4-stage scratch baseline (SimpleCNN) and four transfer-learning configurations (ResNet-18 and ResNet-50 under both frozen-backbone linear probing and full end-to-end fine-tuning) map inputs to 11 class logits. Models are trained with label-smoothed Cross-Entropy Loss (label_smoothing = 0.1), optimized via AdamW with discriminative learning rates, 1-epoch linear warmup + cosine annealing, gradient norm clipping (1.0), and Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP).
  • Prediction, Post-processing & Evaluation: Forward logits are converted in FP32 to class probabilities via softmax and class decisions via argmax. Post-processing includes Expected Calibration Error (ECE, 10 bins) and Grad-CAM visual attribution on layer4[-1]. Dual validation checkpoints (best validation macro_f1 and best validation accuracy) are tracked and evaluated across three independent seeds ([42, 43, 44]), accompanied by paired seed differences, 1,000-iteration bootstrap 95% CIs, and exact McNemar tests.

Experimental setup

To ensure strict experimental fairness and reproducibility across all 15 core training runs (5 model configurations × 3 random seeds), a unified training protocol and predeclared experimental design were enforced.

1. Standardized training setup & hyperparameters

# Setup / Hyperparameter Value / Policy Design Rationale & Protocol Notes
1 Optimizer torch.optim.AdamW (weight_decay = 0.01) Decoupled weight decay regularizes high-capacity backbones effectively during transfer learning and scratch training alike.
2 Learning rate • Classifier head / Scratch: lr_head = 1e-3
• Pretrained backbone: lr_backbone = 1e-4 		Discriminative learning rates allow the randomly initialized classification head to adapt quickly while preventing catastrophic forgetting in pretrained ImageNet weights.
3 Batch size & Input batch_size = 32 at 512 × 512 resolution Consistent across train, validation, and test loaders (drop_last = False). Aspect-preserving Lanczos resize + center train-mean RGB padding preserves food geometry.
4 Epochs Up to 100 epochs (EP_SCRATCH = 100, EP_FT = 100) Matched maximum epoch budget across all five configurations, bounded by dual-criterion early stopping.
5 Scheduler LambdaLR: 1-epoch linear warmup → cosine annealing (min_ratio = 0.01) Stepped per iteration/batch. Warmup stabilizes early gradients in the new linear head before smooth cosine decay to 1% of peak LR.
6 Regularization & Augmentation • Loss: Cross-Entropy with label_smoothing = 0.1
• Head: Dropout(p = 0.3)
• Gradients: Global L2 norm clipping at 1.0
• Train Aug: RandomHorizontalFlip(0.5) + ColorJitter(0.3, 0.3, 0.3, 0.05) 		Mitigates overconfidence on visually ambiguous or noisy web-scraped food labels and improves robustness to lighting/color shifts.
7 Early stopping patience = 10 epochs (shared dual-criterion counter) Patience resets to 0 whenever either validation macro_f1 or validation accuracy improves (ties broken by lower validation loss). Training stops only when neither improves for 10 consecutive epochs.
8 Checkpoint criterion Dual validation checkpoints: best_macro_f1.pt (primary) & best_accuracy.pt Both checkpoints are saved independently on validation and evaluated on the test set in §9. Primary comparisons and plots strictly use best_macro_f1 to account for class imbalance.
9 Seeds & Repeated runs THREE_SEEDS = [42, 43, 44] (3 independent runs / config) Seeds control Python, NumPy, PyTorch, CUDA, cudnn.deterministic=True, and DataLoader shuffling while keeping the official data split invariant. Results reported as mean ± sample std (ddof=1).
10 Hardware & Environment CUDA GPU (Colab / Kaggle environment logged in environment.json) Full environment provenance, SHA-256 split fingerprint (SPLIT_SHA), and run signatures verified automatically before reusing or evaluating checkpoints.
11 Mixed precision Enabled (AMP_ENABLED = True, torch.float16 autocast + GradScaler) Accelerates 512×512 convolutional training and reduces peak VRAM allocation; evaluation loss accumulation is cast back to FP32 for numerical exactness.
12 Training time & Cost tracking Per-epoch & total wall-clock time, Peak GPU VRAM (MiB), GFLOPs, Latency (bs=1) & Throughput (bs=32) Measured via time.perf_counter() (including validation), torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated(), FlopCounterMode (1 MAC = 2 FLOPs), and warmed-up CUDA-synchronized synthetic inference benchmarks.
13 Hyperparameter selection method Proposal-locked controlled recipe (no per-model test tuning) All hyperparameters were fixed a priori in the approved proposal to isolate the effects of pretraining, backbone freezing vs. full fine-tuning, and backbone depth without hyperparameter-search confounding.
